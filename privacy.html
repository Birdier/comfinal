Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect essential transaction information: your name, contact email address, and order history strictly to dispatch download links, invoices, and product release updates.

2. Payment Processing

Financial payment credentials (credit cards, billing accounts) are handled directly via PCI-DSS compliant gateways like Stripe. We never store or transmit sensitive payment keys across our internal servers.

3. Local Browser Storage

Adurite uses browser local storage (`localStorage`) strictly to preserve your interface theme preference and offline cart state. No tracking beacons or intrusive marketing scripts are loaded.

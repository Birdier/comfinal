Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Nature of Digital Goods

Because digital assets, scripts, and design files are immediately accessible upon purchase, standard return mechanisms do not apply. However, customer satisfaction remains paramount.

2. Eligible Refund Criteria

Refunds are granted within 14 days of purchase under the following circumstances:

  • Corrupted archives or file structures that cannot be extracted after support troubleshooting.
  • Substantial discrepancies between documented specifications and actual asset deliverables.
  • Duplicate transactions caused by payment processing timeouts.

3. Resolution Process

Submit inquiries with your transaction invoice ID via our contact portal. Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original funding method within 3 to 5 business days.
